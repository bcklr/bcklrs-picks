01 — Odeal

ODEAL

Lustropolis, 2024

★ 9.9 / 10

Odeal's music is a whole new genre. I loved re-experiencing R&B and hearing a completely new spin on it. When I first heard of him in an interview I was reluctant to check him out — I figured his music wouldn't be much. Little did I know, discovering his music would be one of the best things to happen to me. He speaks on topics so many people can relate to, and he knows how to change flows without ever leaving his original sound. Discovering him was the best thing to happen to my music taste.

02 — Asake

ASAKE

Mr. Money With The Vibe, 2022  ·  M$NEY, 2026

★ 10 / 10    ★ 7.3 / 10

Asake was a crazy discovery. I got into his music while exploring the rising wave of Afrobeats. Like Odeal, he has a truly unique sound — he can make you feel the song before even hearing the lyrics. It's like having your heartbeat revived. Even just hearing his producer tags, whether it's "Tune in to the King of sounds and blues" or "Ololade mi, Asake" — it hits you instantly and you become tuned in. I wasn't fond of him at first, but after watching an interview and hearing more of his sound I became hooked. He easily became one of my top 5 artists of 2026.

03 — Don Toliver

DON
TOLIVER

OCTANE, 2026

★ 8.4 / 10

Rediscovering Don Toliver was an odd experience. You'd hear a lot of negativity around him — people felt his sound hadn't developed past 2019, that he fell off. I can fully admit I wasn't a fan until OCTANE dropped and we got to hear something genuinely different. He stayed true to his sound but brought a completely new vibe. This album is like something that may not look appealing at first but ends up being the most delicious thing on the table. He easily became one of my favorites with just this one project. I hope to hear more soon.

04 — Tems

TEMS

Born in the Wild, 2024  ·  Love Is a Kingdom, 2021

★ 9.3 / 10    ★ 9 / 10

Tems was always played in my household — especially when she featured on "WAIT FOR U." Her part on that song was incredible and really shed light on how talented she is. My favorite album is Love Is a Kingdom purely because those songs are so unique and you don't hear them as much. Her music heavily swayed me toward Afrobeats, as it did for most people. Her music is still going viral to this day. I think that speaks enough for how lit she really is.

05 — Raveena

RAVEENA

Lucid, 2019

★ 10 / 10

I discovered Raveena while trying to become more in touch with my feminine side — she was among my other favorite women artists like Cleo Sol. I chose Raveena because she introduces this slow, loving, intentional type of sound that can bring you to tears. Her music feels soft and cradle-like. It can easily relax your nervous system and it balances out my music taste on the spectrum. You can always tell how intentional she is with her sound just from the melody alone. She's another I hope to hear from again soon.

06 — Kali Uchis

KALI
UCHIS

Red Moon In Venus, 2023

★ 10 / 10

Thee Kali Uchis. I don't remember exactly where I discovered her but she was also found along the path of reconnecting to my feminine side. Her sound is one of the most unique I've heard, and it's not just the sound that keeps people — it's the experiences she sings about. This album in particular was deeply breakup related. I connected with it heavily since I was dealing with something similar at the time. She knows exactly what she's doing and how to perfect a song, especially when she switches her flow mid track. An amazing addition to my music taste.
