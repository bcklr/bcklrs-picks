01 — Odeal

ODEAL

Lustropolis, 2024

9.9 / 10

Odeal's music is a whole new genre. I loved re-experiencing R&B and hearing a completely new spin on it. When I first heard of him in an interview I was reluctant to check him out — I figured his music wouldn't be much. Little did I know, discovering his music would be one of the best things to happen to me. He speaks on so many topics people can relate to, and he knows how to change flows without ever leaving his original sound. Discovering him was the best thing to happen to my music taste.

02 — Asake

ASAKE

Mr. Money With The Vibe, 2022 · M$NEY, 2026

10 / 10 · 7.3 / 10

Asake was a crazy discovery. I got into his music while discovering the rise of Afrobeats. Like Odeal, he has a truly unique sound — he can make you feel the song before even hearing the lyrics. It's like having your heartbeat revived. Even just hearing his producer tags, whether it be "Tune in to the King of sounds and blues" or "Ololade mi, Asake" — it hits you instantly and you become tuned in. It wasn't until I watched an interview and started hearing more of his amazing sound that I became hooked. He easily became one of my top 5 artists of 2026.

03 — Don Toliver

TOLIVER

OCTANE, 2026

8.4 / 10

Rediscovering Don Toliver was an odd experience. You would hear a lot of negativity around this artist — for no real reason. People felt his sound didn't develop past 2019, that he fell off. I can admit I wasn't a fan until OCTANE released and we got to hear his sound really develop. It was amazing how he stayed true to himself but brought a completely different vibe. His music was a game changer for my taste. The OCTANE album is like something that may not look appealing but ends up being the most delicious thing on the table. He easily became one of my favorites with just this one album.

04 — Tems

TEMS

Born In The Wild, 2024 · Love Is A Kingdom, 2021

9.3 / 10 · 9 / 10

Tems was always played in my household, especially when she featured on "WAIT FOR U" — her part on that song was amazing and really shed light on how talented she is. My favorite album is Love Is A Kingdom purely for the fact that the songs within it are so unique and you don't hear them as much. Tems' music means a lot to me as her sound heavily swayed me toward Afrobeats, as it did for most people. Her music is still going viral to this day — I think that speaks enough for how lit she is.

05 — Raveena

RAVEENA

Lucid, 2019

10 / 10

I discovered Raveena while trying to become more in touch with my feminine side. She was among one of my other favorite women artists, Cleo Sol. I chose Raveena because she introduces this slow, loving, intentional type of sound that can bring you to tears. I love her music because it feels soft and cradle-like — it can easily relax your nervous system and it balances out my music taste on the spectrum. You can always tell how intentional she is with her sound just from her melody. She's another I hope to hear from again soon.

06 — Kali Uchis

KALI

Red Moon In Venus, 2023

10 / 10

Thee Kali Uchis. I don't remember where or how I discovered her, but she was one of the artists I found along the path of reconnecting to my feminine side. Her sound is one of the most unique and I won't act like it's all her sound that keeps people coming — it's the experiences she sings about. This album in particular was very breakup related, and I connected deeply with it since I was dealing with something similar at the time. Needless to say, Kali Uchis was an amazing addition to my music taste. She knows exactly what she's doing and how to perfect a song — especially when she switches her flow mid song.
